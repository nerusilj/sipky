## radkovina-beer-mug.png

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: single transparent photo-realistic beer mug sprite for a Czech pub darts-game animation. Primary request: a freshly poured Czech golden lager in one traditional heavy clear 0.5 liter glass beer mug, thick faceted glass with a large comfortable transparent glass handle on the RIGHT. Show the entire mug from a nearly frontal view, only very slightly above so the creamy foam's top is visible as a narrow oval. The mug stands perfectly upright, perfectly vertical. Authentic fine dense white beer foam, a small irregular natural foam crest, NOT cartoon bubbles; golden amber beer with fine rising bubbles inside, cool condensation beads on the glass. Subtle glass refraction, sharply readable thick glass base and realistic clear handle opening. Warm light from upper left plus subtle neutral edge light from right, meant to sit on a dark warm pub background. Product photography, refined studio realism. Composition portrait 3:4 or 2:3, full mug fills almost all canvas height with about 4 percent transparent padding at top and bottom, no huge margins. The glass body center should be centered slightly LEFT of the canvas center to allow room for right handle. Genuine transparent alpha background outside glass, including handle hole. NO coaster, no table, no environment, no labels, no words, no logos, no extra items, no hand, no cut-off pieces, no cast background shadow. A single realistic physical mug only; not vector, not illustration. Keep foam natural and contained, no spillage down the front.

## radkovina-coaster.png

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: blank beer coaster transparent texture sprite for a Czech pub game. Generate a single round BEER COASTER / beer mat made of warm natural pale tan thick absorbent pressed paper pulp with tiny visible fibers and slightly porous matte surface. Completely blank central area so a dynamic dark printed number can be added later by software. Authentic subtle paper grain, fine tiny imperfections, a faint small partial damp beer ring near the outer edge, lightly worn edge. The object must be a perfectly circular, thin flat cardboard disk, photographed exactly from directly overhead (90 degree top down), without perspective foreshortening. Flat paper mat, NOT a plate or bowl, NO raised rim, no concentric molded ridges. Thin edge visible only as a hairline. Warm soft upper-left lighting consistent with a pub table scene but no visible table. Square composition, the round disk centered and occupying 92 percent of canvas width and height, genuinely transparent alpha outside the circle. No labels, no text, no numbers, no brand, no logo, no glass, no beer, no other objects, no big cast shadow. Photorealistic product photo suitable for texture mapping onto a perspective ellipse in an animated game.
